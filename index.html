A MESSAGE FROM YOUR PENGUIN

Dear Fatima, 💌

Happy Birthday, Fatima! 🥹💕

Sometimes I wonder how a friendship can become filled with so many little moments that stay in your heart.

I still think about our hand-holding story. There was a time when you would hold my hand and I would let it go. And look at us now! I'm the one asking for your hand first. 😂 Who would have thought things would turn out this way?

And honestly, I'm still learning how to handle my jealousy. Sometimes I go quiet, sometimes I tease you, and sometimes I pretend nothing bothers me. But you probably notice more than I think you do. Those moments when you hold my hand a little tighter mean more to me than you realise.

I also remember when you told me, "Love exist kerta hai." Maybe you don't know how much that sentence made me think. I have my own way of believing in love, and our friendship has given me so many little moments to think about what it means to care for someone.

And my favourite little identity in our story? Penguin. 🐧 You chose that nickname for me yourself, and now it's something that makes me smile whenever I think about it.

I asked about your favourite colours, rabbits, and favourite song because I wanted this little surprise to include things you love. I wanted to make something that feels a little more like you.

I don't know what the future holds or how life will change, but I genuinely hope it brings you beautiful things. May you achieve everything you dream of, laugh without holding back, and always feel valued.

Thank you for the laughs, the teasing, the hand-holding, the little moments, and even the moments when my jealousy gets the better of me. 😂

I also want to tell you something honestly. I have never said that you are jealous, but whenever you say, "I am never jealous," it hurts me the most. I never understand why you keep telling me that. Is it because you don't want me to have these kinds of expectations from you? I genuinely want to understand what you mean because those words make me feel bad.

And I'm truly sorry for the rude words I used last night. I know I hurt you, and I regret speaking that way. Whatever I was feeling, I should have expressed it more gently. You didn't deserve to be hurt by my words. Please accept my sincere apology.

Happy Birthday once again, Fatima. Stay happy, stay a little crazy, and never stop being you.

With lots of love,
Your Penguin 🐧💗
